About the App

Know your exact net worth in minutes — no bank account linking required. Just enter your assets and liabilities manually, and see your net worth calculated instantly. Your data never leaves your phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Do I need to link my bank account?

No. The app is entirely manual. You enter your asset and liability values yourself. No bank login, no account linking — ever.

Q. Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Your financial data never leaves your phone.

Q. How do monthly snapshots work?

Once per month, when you open the app, a snapshot of your current net worth is automatically saved. This builds your historical chart over time.

Q. Why are ads shown?

The app is 100% free with no subscription. Ads via Google AdMob help support continued development and maintenance.
