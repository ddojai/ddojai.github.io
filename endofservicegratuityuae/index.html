Frequently asked questions

Does this cover every worker in the UAE?

No. It is limited to the supported monthly-paid foreign private-sector full-time case. Unknown or excluded arrangements need official or qualified advice.

Is the estimate my final settlement?

No. It is not legal advice or a finding about deductions, disputes or entitlement. Confirm with HR, MOHRE or a qualified adviser.

What happens at the cap boundary?

The app shows that the cap needs confirmation rather than presenting the raw calculation as a confirmed final amount.
