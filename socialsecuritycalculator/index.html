About

Compare estimated monthly and cumulative Social Security benefits for two claiming ages. Change the comparison end age and see where cumulative totals cross.

Version

This page describes version 1.1.0 after its App Store release. Availability depends on the version installed; earlier versions follow the version-specific information in the privacy policy.

Features

Two claiming ages from 62 to 70
Statement-based FRA amount or an educational income example
Up to three local scenarios and share preview

Frequently asked questions

Is this an SSA app or a personal benefit quote?

No. It is an independent educational estimator. Confirm your personal estimate and eligibility at ssa.gov.

Does the cumulative total include taxes and Medicare?

The main comparison excludes taxes, Medicare premiums, investment returns and discounting. Separate examples are not added to it.

Does optional COLA predict future increases?

No. The optional 2.5% annual COLA is a comparison assumption and starts off each time the app opens.
